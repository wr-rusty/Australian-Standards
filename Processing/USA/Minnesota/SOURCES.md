# Sources — Minnesota (MnDOT)

* `Original PDFs/` — MnDOT Standard Signs and Markings Manual sections (R, M, S, OM, X from the 2026 phased release;
  W, G, D, I, E from 2023–24; border/radii, arrows, symbols; the Standard Signs Summary), downloaded from
  https://www.dot.state.mn.us/trafficeng/publ/signsmanual/index.html (edocs-public.dot.state.mn.us links).
  The manual is a supplement to the FHWA SHS with Minnesota-specific designs and modified federal designs.
