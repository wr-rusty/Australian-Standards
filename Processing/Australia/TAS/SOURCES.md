* The drawings are black line-work CAD sheets (colours and letter series stated in the notes, several drawn sideways on
  portrait pages). Line-work extraction does not give usable faces (see the UK attempt), so this pack is to be built
  spec-driven from the drawings, the way AS 1743 and the NSW rebuild are done: 15 sign drawings — 3403-3/P70-2, P71-2,
  P92, P93, P94, 3403-5/P406-1 (fingerboards, several), P411-1, P514, G/9/351B, G/10/444A, 445A, 457, 469B, 474, 484,
  649 (several panels).
* Otherwise Tasmania applies AS 1742/1743: the national pack covers its signs.

## Generated set (`SVGs (generated)/`)

Built spec-driven on 2026-09-08 (SGN-006), the way the NSW rebuild is done: one spec per sheet in `tools/specs/TAS/<CODE>.json`
(top-level `"pack": "TAS"`, `"drawing"` naming the sheet where the sign has its own code), every dimension read from the sheet
(rendered upright at 200 dpi into `Original PNGs/<sheet>.png` by `tools/trace_symbol.py --render-pngs TAS`; the sideways
portrait sheets with `--turn -90`), generated by `tools/signgen.py` into `SVGs (generated)/<family>/` with `MANIFEST.csv`.
Symbols traced from the sheets: `tools/symbols/tas_w6-101_bus_child.svg` (school bus signs), `tas_g9-101_truck.svg`;
the slippery car on the ice/grit/gravel signs is the AS 1743 W5-20 symbol the sheets refer to. Overlay-checked with
`tools/compare_drawing.py` (specs carry `panel_px`).

* 15 SVGs: 3403-3/P70-2, P71-2 (B size), P92, W5-118 (P93), T1-105 (P94), 3403-5/P514, W5-103, W6-101, W6-102,
  T1-101A, T1-101B, W5-102, R4-1(50)SZ, G/10/649 panels A and B.
* G9-101 (G/10/484): spec written, excluded from generation as the national G9 signs are (Guide Signs).
* Skipped: 3403-5/P406-1 (fingerboard templates), 3403-5/P411-1 (border/arrow/shield details), G/10/649 optional safety
  messages (logos). The 3403-9 sheets are mounting details, not rendered.
* Letter spacing: AS 1744 (plus0) as in the national pack. The TAS sheets' "medium"/"75 %" spacing runs about 7 % wider,
  so the manifest's width checks flag most words; the specs' notes give both figures.
* Reuse: the sheets are Crown copyright, State of Tasmania (Department of State Growth, formerly DIER). The site's
  copyright page (transport.tas.gov.au/copyright) sits behind the same bot wall as the drawings and could not be read
  on 2026-09-08 — terms to be confirmed before redistribution; credit line in `tools/signgen.py` PACKS.
