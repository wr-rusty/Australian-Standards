# Sources — Queensland (TMR)

* `Original PDFs/TC signs/` — TMR "TC signs full collection" (June 2026), the officially approved non-standard traffic
  control signs, one A4 design sheet per sign in category folders, plus TMR's index. Downloaded from
  https://www.tmr.qld.gov.au/business-industry/Technical-standards-publications/TC-signs (updated quarterly; the site
  refuses scripted downloads, so the ZIP was fetched through the Browser pane).
* `Original PDFs/q-series.pdf` — MUTCD Q-series signs (Queensland-specific signs in Part 1 of the Queensland MUTCD); not yet extracted.
* `Original PDFs/qld-mutcd-part-1.pdf`, `amendments.pdf` — Queensland MUTCD Part 1 (sign illustrations) and the TC signs amendment list.
* Licence: Queensland Government publications are under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 unless stated otherwise on the
  document; the TC sign sheets carry no separate restriction. Attribute "© State of Queensland (Department of Transport and Main Roads)".

`SVGs/` is produced by `tools/qld_extract.py` (see `tools/README.md`); `SVGs/MANIFEST.csv` records the size source
and anything to check. Superseded sheets, the index, logo sheets and the signing/pavement layout sheets are skipped.
