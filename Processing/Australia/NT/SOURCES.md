## Generated set (`SVGs (generated)/`)

Built spec-driven on 2026-09-08 (SGN-007): one spec per sheet in `tools/specs/NT/<CODE>.json` (`"pack": "NT"`,
`"drawing"` = sheet file stem), sheets rendered at 200 dpi into `Original PNGs/` (`tools/trace_symbol.py --render-pngs NT`,
the landscape CS35xx sheets with `--turn 0`), generated by `tools/signgen.py` into `SVGs (generated)/<family>/` with
`MANIFEST.csv`; `tools/symbols/nt_cs3507_truck.svg` traced from CS3507; the W3-3 signal pictogram on CS1557 is the AS 1743
symbol. Overlay-checked with `tools/compare_drawing.py`.

* 8 SVGs: CS1557 (traffic signal advance warning board), CS3504 at 60/80/100/110 km/h, CS3505 (ROAD CLOSED 2400 x 600),
  CS3507 types A and B (compression braking).
* Pointing at the national pack (manifest rows, no SVG): CS3503 (W6-3 children), CS3509 layouts (R4-1, R4-11), CS3511-3513
  (G7-3-1, G7-3-2, G7-1-2, G7-4-2), CS3514 (G11-1, G11-2, G11-4, G11-7), CS3516/3517 (2 x D4-1-1).
* Skipped: CS3503's school-zone panel and CS3506 truck rest area (not dimensioned / drawn not to scale), CS3510-3514 NT
  message plates (TraSiCAD templates with placeholder names, "dimensions to be finalised"), CS3518 hardware, CS3451/3459/
  CS-3460..3463 project signs and CS-3519..3521 advertising signs (structures with "insert image here" panels).
