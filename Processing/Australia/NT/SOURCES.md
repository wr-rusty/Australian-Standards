# Sources — Northern Territory (Department of Logistics and Infrastructure)

* Civil standard drawings, https://dli.nt.gov.au/industry/technical-standards-guidelines-and-specifications/standard-drawings
  — series "Road furniture and signs CS 3500–3599" (school zone sign details CS3503, NT standard speed limit sign CS3504,
  road closure sign CS3505, truck parking bay sign CS3506, limited compression braking information sign CS3507, highway
  service / rest area / tourist advance signage CS3510–3514, hazard markers CS3516–3518, advertising signs CS3519–3521),
  "Traffic control devices CS 3400–3449" and "Project signs CS 3450–3499"; also CS1557 traffic signal advance warning sign.
* The site sits behind a JavaScript bot check: curl (even with the browser's cookies) gets the check page, and the
  browser pane hands each PDF to a save dialog. Download them by hand (or click through the pane's dialogs) into
  `Original PDFs/`, then run the sheet extractor.
* Otherwise the NT applies AS 1742/1743: the national pack covers its signs.
