# Sources — Victoria (Department of Transport and Planning)

* `Original PDFs/TEM-Vol-2-Part-2.17-AS1743-Road-Signs-Specifications-_v2_.pdf` — DTP "Network Technical Guide
  Supplement to AS 1743:2023 Road signs – Specifications" v2.0 (Traffic Engineering Manual Vol 2 Part 2.17, 2024):
  370 pages, one Victorian V-series sign drawing per page (R, W, G, GE, D, T series, e.g. R2-V110) with index tables
  of sign number / description / drawing number. Vector CAD sheets drawn reading up the page.
* `Original PDFs/TEM-Vol-3-Part-2.12-Tourist-and-Services-Signs.pdf` — TEM Vol 3 Part 2.12 (G11-V / G7-V tourist and
  service sign drawings, 2014).
* Also downloaded for reference: TEM Vol 2 Parts 2.01 and 2.05, TEM Vol 3 Part 2.14.
* Found through the DTP Engineering Standards Catalogue (`Engineering-Standards-Catalogue-August-2026.xlsm` on
  content.vic.gov.au, linked from https://www.vic.gov.au/dtp-technical-publications).
* Licence: Victorian Government technical publication; DTP terms of use apply (check before commercial redistribution).

`SVGs/` is produced by `tools/vic_extract.py` (sheet extractor, page by page, every page turned 90°); names come from the
books' index tables; sizes from the drawings' dimension figures where readable, else drawn at 1:10 and marked "check".
