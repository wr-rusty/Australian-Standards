## Summary

SA's symbol diamonds and all of Temporary, Hazard, Service and Symbols (about 390 signs) were built from the sheets' filled vector artwork with legends re-set in the FHWA fonts, not from the stated dimensions. Overlays score well but the chains were read only at thumbnail scale, so a sheet whose drawing is off its own figures would be reproduced as drawn.

## Evidence

- Tickets/SGN-005 Log 2026-10-02; each affected spec's notes say 'built from the sheet's artwork'.

## Fix

For each such spec compare size, border, letter heights/series and the main chain against the sheet's figures; correct where the drawing and figures disagree (figures win unless they do not sum).

## Verify

Notes in each spec record 'chain checked <date>'; mismatches fixed or explained.
