## Summary

Japan's ministry (MLIT) publishes the whole sign list as one vector PDF: about 250 signs in five classes, each labelled with its official number. No sizes on the sheet; they are in the sign order (標識令).

## Evidence

- https://www.mlit.go.jp/road/sign/sign/douro/ichiran.pdf (checked 2026-10-02: 1 page, 1,893 vector paths, no images).
- https://www.mlit.go.jp/road/sign/sign/ (sign order tables, installation standard).

## Fix

Record the reuse terms; lift each sign's artwork by its number label; sizes from the sign order or nominal and marked; pack in Processing/Japan/National (MLIT).
