## Summary

Where a sheet gives no list, variants are the sheet's example plus values in usual practice. None are confirmed against the state's practice; some sets are probably too wide.

## Evidence

- NSW: T1-273n UHF 40/29; R6-244n 3/5/10/15/20 t; OSOM and G7-218n distances 200/300/500 m and 1/2/5 km.
- QLD: tramway 10-60; heavy vehicle 40-100; bicycles present 30-60; plate 10-110; W5-040-Q01 2/5/10 km.
- SA: R6-SA12 clearance 3.0-5.0 m in 0.1 steps (21 files — likely cut to the example); R4-SA61A END 40/50 AREA (sheet shows no numeral); distances 100-500 m, 1/2/3/5/10 km; exit speed 30-80; tram 10-50; delays 5-30 min.
- NT: CS3504 60/80/100/110.

## Fix

Russell (or the inspector) marks which values stay; specs trimmed and regenerated.

## Verify

Each vary spec's notes say 'values confirmed <date>'.
